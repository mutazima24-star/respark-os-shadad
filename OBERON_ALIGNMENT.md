## Oberon visual vocabulary observed

### Color system
- Dominant background: warm off-white / paper: `#efedeb`, `#eeecea`, `#f2f0ee`.
- Secondary surfaces: pale sand / grey-beige: `#dcdbd5`, `#e4e3e0`, `#d8d7d2`.
- Primary ink: near black: `#1a1a1a`, `#222222`, `#2a2827`.
- Accent: restrained orange/red-orange, used for CTAs, numbering, small chart accents.
- Palette posture: low saturation, editorial/industrial; no blue/purple SaaS gradients.

### Typography
- Large compressed editorial headlines with tight negative tracking.
- Small uppercase mono labels for section markers and UI/status language.
- Body copy is restrained, mid-grey, and secondary to layout.

### Composition
- Operations-first surface, not generic hero/cards.
- Light paper grid backgrounds with vertical/horizontal hairlines.
- Black rounded panels placed over light canvas.
- Mixed asymmetry: a large editorial block beside device/card mockups.
- Sections are numbered: `.01`, `.02`, `.03` etc.
- CTA is often a solid orange or black block, not glossy gradient.

### Motifs
- Halftone/dithered black-and-white imagery.
- Thin line icons and small operational diagrams.
- Card stacks, case-study cards, queue/process rows.
- Simple arrows, dashboard snippets, bars/mini charts.
- Motion should feel like operational reveal/scroll, not theatrical animation.
